# Bearing — Editorial Supply Proof

**Status:** active prerequisite within the current second-story proof.

Before selecting the second production story, prove that Bearing has a sufficiently deep and well-ranked current story supply.

Read and follow:
- `product/EDITORIAL_STORY_LIBRARY.md`
- `product/NATIONAL_ASSIGNMENT_DESK.md`
- `product/COVERAGE_RADAR.md`
- `product/PRODUCT_VISION.md`
- `product/NEWSROOM_ARCHITECTURE.md`

## Problem to solve

The existing national desk can gather hundreds of reports but often exposes too few usable current story threads for a newsroom to cycle through naturally.

Do not solve this by padding Top Stories.

Build or refine the **Current Story Library** between canonical event intake and ranking.

## Required proof

Using the live connected source set:

1. Audit the current funnel:
   - total canonical story threads,
   - active/current/continuing,
   - developing,
   - watch,
   - held/out of scope,
   - top-ranked.
2. Sample excluded and Watch stories to identify why legitimate national stories are not reaching the usable library.
3. Separate **current-story eligibility** from **priority ranking**.
4. Add explicit Bearing strategy dimensions and inspectable reasons.
5. Support consequential **continuing** stories without pretending they are newly published today.
6. Reassess Watch/Held material when new evidence resolves the blocker.
7. Produce a deep ranked current-story library, normally targeting roughly 20–60 legitimate active/current/continuing story threads when the live news environment and connected sources support that depth.
8. Keep Top Stories small and selective; it should draw from the larger library.
9. Verify that the ranked library includes a useful range of national consequence: safety/hazards, economy/household, health, government/courts/accountability, technology/infrastructure, consequential world news and other major developments when present.
10. Compare a sample of the live output against major current national stories observable from connected reporting and record obvious misses and why they were missed.

Do not lower evidence standards merely to increase count.

## Then select the second story

Once the current library is robust enough to provide genuine editorial choice, select a materially different second story **from that library** and continue the existing Second Story + Graphics Quality Proof.

The production story should not be hand-inserted merely because it is convenient for testing.

## Stop / reporting

At the second-story private review, include:
- before/after usable story-library depth,
- examples of stories newly retained because eligibility and ranking were separated,
- examples correctly left in Watch/Held,
- the strategy dimensions that changed ranking,
- obvious live-news misses that remain,
- the reason the selected second story ranked strongly enough to produce.

Do not claim national completeness. The connected source set still has documented gaps.
