# U.S. editorial selection and camera tiles

Checked September 29, 2026. Prioritize consumer costs, economic effects and concrete policy actions for an American audience. Title-based rules suppress political sparring without a substantive action, diversify publishers and cap international publishers at roughly 10% of a full selection. This is a ranking heuristic, not a factual or bias review. A measured 32-story sample contained 29 U.S.-publisher stories.

Verified public discovery feeds include Axios, CNBC, NPR business, PBS economy and FTC consumer protection. Axios redirects to api.axios.com/feed/. AP's tested RSS endpoint returned 403; CNN's legacy HTTPS feeds failed verification. The user approved AP/CNN as prominent source links pending licensed access. Public RSS availability does not grant republication rights to full articles or media. See [AP API access](https://api.ap.org/media/v/docs/Getting_Started_API.htm).

## Camera presentation

Two separate homepage tiles use official YouTube privacy-enhanced embeds. They are not inserted into the news playlist or extracted into an outgoing broadcast. Only one camera opens at a time; opening it pauses the main channel. A permanent source link is available if embedding fails.

- **USGS Kilauea V1**, observation camera. Current provider link https://url.usgs.gov/v1cam resolved to HggWKlZv9yk. [The official camera page](https://www.usgs.gov/media/webcams/kilauea-volcano-live-stream-halemaumau-crater) explicitly marks this camera public domain. Credit USGS/Hawaiian Volcano Observatory. Its picture alone is not an activity alert.
- **NASA ISS Earth view**, beauty shot with no implied connection to the current story. Current official stream awQzjn72bI0 is linked through the Space Station Views playlist on [NASA Live](https://www.nasa.gov/live/). [NASA's media policy](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/) permits factual editorial use of NASA imagery with attribution and third-party exceptions. NASA may show footage marked Previously Recorded; do not describe every frame as live.

Official embeds follow [YouTube's embed guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/171780?hl=en). Embedding permission is not unrestricted permission to extract or relay a YouTube stream. Future rebroadcast needs an authorized media delivery source and asset-level rights checks. Camera IDs can rotate; these are verified current listings, not permanent uptime guarantees. Records and policy links are in channel/dist/cameras.json.
