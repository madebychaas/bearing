# Easy News → Bearing — Selection-to-Production Proof

## Mission

Prove one continuous editorial chain from a producer-selected Easy News story opportunity to a finished Bearing asset.

The goal is not to build a larger platform. The goal is to stop making the production side rediscover editorial context the intelligence side already knows.

**Evolve, don't rebuild. Prove, don't platformize. Produced, not generated.**

## Product behavior to prove

For one real, current story:

1. Easy News surfaces the story opportunity and explains why it matters.
2. A producer selects an appropriate product treatment.
3. The story's source evidence, current news peg, why-now reasoning, product intent, and relevant editorial context carry forward into the production workflow.
4. The draft/edit step uses the maintained Bearing editorial voice standard.
5. The producer can review, revise, approve, hold, or reject the script before production.
6. Bearing produces the finished asset using the existing picture, sound, timing, source, rights, and playback quality gates.
7. The finished asset remains traceable to the reporting and editorial decision that created it.
8. If the underlying reporting materially changes before completion, the workflow requires editorial reassessment rather than silently finishing stale work.

## What this mission should preserve

- the existing Easy News producer desk,
- the current reporting intake and source-grounding behavior,
- the maintained editorial voice profile and rewrite examples,
- the accepted Bearing film-production machinery,
- continuous playback,
- provenance and rights checks,
- human editorial control.

## Production quality requirements

Carry forward the lessons from the accepted PCE and cybersecurity work:

- do not solve length by making narration faster,
- protect natural sentence breaths and the first word,
- give graphics enough dwell time to be understood,
- reveal facts in the order the narration needs them,
- change visual composition when the editorial task changes,
- use music and sound as support, not camouflage,
- make every visual beat explain something,
- keep claims bounded by the evidence,
- end on the specific consequence, uncertainty, decision, deadline, or next hinge that actually matters.

The 13 rewritten scripts are learning examples, not a production backlog.

## Human control

A surfaced Easy News candidate is not an assignment.

A producer decision is not automatic publication.

The workflow may recommend, draft, prepare, and produce, but editorial selection and approval must remain inspectable and reversible.

## Technical freedom

These are behavioral requirements, not implementation instructions.

Inspect the existing codebase and choose the smallest clean path that connects the working pieces.

Reuse existing machinery wherever practical.

Generalize only what this proof requires.

Do not stop for routine engineering choices.

## Explicit non-goals

Do not use this mission to:

- render all rewritten scripts,
- build a generalized CMS,
- create a full assignment-management system,
- redesign Bearing,
- add broad autonomous publication,
- create a multi-agent newsroom,
- build generalized multi-profile infrastructure,
- or refactor working systems solely for architectural elegance.

## Success test

The proof succeeds when a producer can take one current Easy News opportunity, choose the product treatment, carry the editorial understanding forward, review the script, and receive a finished Bearing asset without the system having to reconstruct the story from scratch.

The resulting asset should preserve:

- source grounding,
- why-now context,
- product intent,
- editorial decisions,
- approval state,
- and the relationship between the final asset and the underlying reporting.

Test one material-change case as part of the proof. A changed story should trigger reassessment rather than stale completion.

## Stop condition

When the end-to-end proof works:

1. test it with a real or representative current story,
2. document what context traveled cleanly and what still had to be reconstructed,
3. commit and push the stable state,
4. update the handoff,
5. stop.

Do not broaden the build without a new mission.
